# Vocaband subscriptions in Israel

| Plan | Price in ILS | Capacity | Included |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | ₪0 | One class, up to 30 approved students and three open assignments | QR joining, core activities and assignment results |
| Teacher | ₪12 for one month or ₪99 for one year | 60 approved students across the teacher's active classes | Personal vocabulary library, unlimited assignments and longitudinal progress reports |
| Teacher Pro | ₪19 for one month or ₪169 for one year | 150 approved students across the teacher's active classes | Assign a saved set to multiple classes, plus OCR and AI generation |
| School | ₪2,490 for one year | Six teachers and 400 approved students | Pro access for licensed teachers, shared vocabulary library, assignments across the team, coordinator CSV reports and central billing |
| School pilot | ₪590 for six weeks | Two teachers and up to 400 approved students | School features for the pilot term. Apply the full pilot payment to the first annual purchase, leaving ₪1,900 due. |

Teacher Pro expands in blocks of 50 students at ₪6 for a monthly term or ₪49 for an annual term. Annual Pro for 200 students costs ₪218. An expansion or eligible personal plan upgrade during an active term charges the prorated price difference and keeps the original expiration date.

Each price covers the stated plan and term. The quote counts each approved student account once within its license scope across active classes. Pending profiles and archived classes are excluded. Paid terms are prepaid and renewed explicitly. The 14-day teacher trial includes Pro features without a card and falls back to Free at expiry. The Free limits govern new use after expiry. Existing assignments and progress remain available to the teacher in the gradebook.

Teacher Pro, active school licenses and trials include a combined allowance of 30 requests per teacher per Israel calendar month for the metered AI and OCR features. AI cache hits on those features do not use the allowance. Each class retains ownership of its own students and assignment results. The coordinator report contains class-level completion totals.

See the [subscriptions and notices runbook](teacher-billing-and-notices.md) for the merchant configuration and staging acceptance checks. The server verifies provider payments and recalculates each quote when reserving the order. The displayed price is shared by the public pricing section and teacher subscription dialog.
