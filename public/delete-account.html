Delete your account

Applies to the Vocaband student and teacher apps (com.vocaband.student, web app at vocaband.com).
You can permanently delete your Vocaband account and its data at any time — either yourself from inside the app, or by asking us. There is no charge.

Option 1 — Delete it yourself (immediate)

  1. Open the Vocaband app (or sign in at vocaband.com).
  2. Students: sign in with your class as usual. Teachers: sign in to your account.
  3. Go to Settings → Privacy Settings.
  4. Tap “Delete My Account” and confirm.

Your account and data are deleted right away and you are signed out.

Option 2 — Ask us to delete it

Email privacy@vocaband.com from the account’s email address (or, for a child, a parent or guardian may write on their behalf). Tell us the account email so we can find it. We complete verified deletion requests within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: display name, email address, and login history.
  • Your game progress: XP, streaks, titles, avatars, and results.
  • Your student or teacher profile record and consent records.
  • For a teacher account: the classes you own, their assignments, and the linked student records cascade with it.

What is kept, and for how long

For security and legal reasons a small amount of data outlives the account, then deletes automatically:

DataKept forWhy
Security audit-log entries (e.g. login, deletion events)Up to ~2 years (730 days), then auto-deletedLegal-retention exemption (GDPR Art. 17(3)(b)/(e))
Encrypted database backupsUp to 30 days, then overwrittenProvider point-in-time recovery rotation
Off-site disaster-recovery archive (encrypted)Up to 365 days, then auto-deletedBackup lifecycle policy

Backup copies are encrypted and are not used except for disaster recovery; they are overwritten or deleted automatically at the end of the windows above.

Questions? Contact privacy@vocaband.com. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

מחיקת החשבון

אפשר למחוק את חשבון Vocaband ואת כל הנתונים שלו בכל עת, ללא תשלום.

אפשרות 1 — מחיקה עצמית (מיידית):

  1. פותחים את האפליקציה או נכנסים לאתר vocaband.com ומתחברים.
  2. עוברים אל הגדרות ← הגדרות פרטיות.
  3. לוחצים על ”מחיקת החשבון שלי“ ומאשרים.

אפשרות 2 — בקשה מאיתנו: שולחים מייל אל privacy@vocaband.com מכתובת המייל של החשבון (הורה יכול לפנות עבור ילדו). נטפל בבקשה תוך 30 יום.

יומני אבטחה נשמרים עד כשנתיים ועותקי גיבוי מוצפנים עד 30–365 יום, ואז נמחקים אוטומטית. פרטים מלאים במדיניות הפרטיות.

حذف حسابك

يمكنك حذف حساب Vocaband وجميع بياناته في أي وقت ومجانًا.

الطريقة 1 — الحذف بنفسك (فوري):

  1. افتح التطبيق أو سجّل الدخول عبر vocaband.com.
  2. اذهب إلى الإعدادات ← إعدادات الخصوصية.
  3. اضغط ”حذف حسابي“ وأكِّد.

الطريقة 2 — اطلب منّا الحذف: راسِلنا على privacy@vocaband.com من بريد الحساب (يمكن لوليّ الأمر المراسلة نيابةً عن الطفل). ننفّذ الطلب خلال 30 يومًا.

تُحفَظ سجلات الأمان حتى نحو سنتين والنُّسخ الاحتياطية المشفَّرة حتى 30–365 يومًا ثم تُحذَف تلقائيًا. التفاصيل في سياسة الخصوصية.
